<h1># Buildables Fellowship Repository</h1>

This repository documents my journey and projects from the **12-week intensive Cybersecurity + AI Fellowship with Buildables**.  
During this fellowship, I had the opportunity to work closely with **company-level mentors**, collaborate with peers, and build a variety of hands-on projects that strengthened my technical, problem-solving, and teamwork skills.  

---

<h3>## 📅 Program Overview</h3>

- **Fellowship Start Date**: 18/08/2025  
- **Fellowship End Date**: 22/11/2025  
- **Duration**: 12 Weeks, full-time, intensive  

The **Comprehensive Cybersecurity + AI Fellowship** is designed to transform motivated graduates into cybersecurity professionals with cutting-edge expertise in **defensive, offensive, and governance (GRC) domains**, augmented by the power of **AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Agentic AI systems**.  

### Fellowship Highlights
- Foundations to advanced mastery of **cybersecurity principles**  
- Training in **red team / blue team strategies**  
- AI-powered **threat detection** and **agentic AI applications**  
- **Portfolio development**:
  - 15+ Labs  
  - 60+ Projects (mini + capstone)  
  - Research paper / technical report suitable for conference or journal submission  
- Preparation for industry certifications:
  - **CompTIA Security+**  
  - **CEH (Certified Ethical Hacker)**  
- Career readiness for:
  - SOC Operations  
  - Penetration Testing  
  - Cyber Threat Intelligence  
  - AI-driven Security Research  
  - Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC)
